Tennis is a racket sport that can be played by two people or two teams of two. Tennis is an olympic sport and hobby that can be played by anyone as long as they can hold the racket. The game originated in Birmingham England in the late 19th century, it had close connections such as croquet and bowls as well as to some older racket sports. Today it is called real tennis. history

Research cannot trace the exact origin but the most common view is that it was invented in the 11th or 12th century by french monks. The nobles modified their courtyards to indoor courts and developed bats to hit balls, which were made of corks wrapped in string or cloth and later leather. By 1500 a wooden frame racket was commonly used. Together with a cork the ball weighed around three ounces. Tennis had some royal fans including Henry VII and Henry VIII who built more courts(including one of the few surviving courts built at Hampton Palace in 1625 ). The origin of today's game goes back to Major Harry Gem and his friend Augurio Perera, who combined elements of the game of racket and the Spansh ball game called Pelota, playing it outside on a crouquet lawn. Major Walter Clopton Wingfeild, who is widely accepted as the inventor of the mordern game of tennis, further developed the game in Decemberr 1873 and created a new variation called Sphairistrike. That name was derived from the Ancient ball meaning 'skill at playing ball'. He also invented the equipment and published the rules.

tips

The 3R's in tennis are: react, respond, recover. Go for depth instead of trying to hit the ball super hard. Hit balls with your preferred side. Focus on depth, height, direction, speed, and spin
